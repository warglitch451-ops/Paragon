Since 1939

A LEGACY SERVED
WITH LOVE.

Rooted in the rich culinary traditions of the Malabar coast, Paragon has been a beacon of authentic taste for over eight decades. What started as a humble establishment in Kozhikode has evolved into an international symbol of culinary excellence.

Our commitment remains unchanged: to source the finest ingredients, preserve time-honored recipes, and serve every guest with the warmth of traditional Indian hospitality.
